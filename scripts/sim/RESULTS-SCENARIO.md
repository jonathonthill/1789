# 1789 — the narrowed question

_40,000 games per cell (±0.5 points). Every cell plays the same decks._

Held fixed at every table: **two Pamphleteers, exposed** (no protection from the
counterattack), **exact kills won into the slayer's hand**, **one card of spoils**
per fallen royal, and hands of **8 / 7 / 6 / 5**. The only things varying are
whether the Pamphleteer may bring a companion, and how many Regroups the table
is granted.

The four tables differ in who is playing. **Average** is the headline: it plays
exactly as the reference bot does — hears the table, weighs every legal action —
but judges by hand-reasoned instinct rather than fitted weights. **Quiet** hears
no remarks and takes the biggest safe swing. **Good** is the fitted reference.
The last is one weaker citoyen seated among average ones.
