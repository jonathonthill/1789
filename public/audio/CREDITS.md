# Audio credits

`do-you-hear-the-people-sing.mid` is the copyright-owner-authorized free demo
of “Do You Hear the People Sing” (Claude-Michel Schönberg), downloaded from
[MIDI DB](https://www.mididb.com/les-miserables-musical/do-you-hear-the-people-sing-midi/).

MIDI DB states that its free MIDI files are demo arrangements and that the
copyright owners have granted permission to download them. The bundled file is
the demo only; the full professional arrangement is available from Hit Trax.
